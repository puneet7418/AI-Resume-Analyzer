Job Title: Junior Data Analyst
Company: TechCorp India, Noida

About the Role:
We are looking for a Junior Data Analyst to join our growing analytics team.
You will work with cross-functional teams to turn raw data into actionable insights.

Responsibilities:
- Write complex SQL queries to extract and analyze large datasets
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI and Tableau
- Conduct A/B testing and statistical analysis to support product decisions
- Prepare ad hoc reports for stakeholders
- Automate reporting workflows using Python

Requirements:
- Proficiency in SQL (mandatory)
- Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Strong data visualization and storytelling skills
- Familiarity with Excel and pivot tables
- Good communication skills

Nice to Have:
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP)
- Knowledge of dbt or BigQuery
- Exposure to machine learning concepts
